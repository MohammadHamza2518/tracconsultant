## 4. Professional CA Financial Tools Suite (8 Tools)

1. **HRA Exemption Calculator (`/tools/hra-calculator`)**:
   - Computes legal HRA exemption under Section 10(13A).
   - Side-by-side comparison of Metro (50%) vs Non-Metro (40%), rent paid minus 10% basic salary, and actual HRA received.
2. **Advance Tax Calculator (`/tools/advance-tax-calculator`)**:
   - Computes 4 mandatory quarterly installments (15% by Jun 15, 45% by Sep 15, 75% by Dec 15, 100% by Mar 15).
   - Evaluates interest penalties under Section 234B and Section 234C.
3. **Old vs New Tax Regime Calculator (`/tools/tax-calculator`)**:
   - Updated for Union Budget AY 2025–26.
   - Evaluates ₹75,000 standard deduction, Section 87A rebate up to ₹7.75 Lakhs, and 80C/80D deductions.
   - Provides clear recommendation on which tax regime yields maximum savings.

### 4.2 Paid Professional Accounting & Audit Tools (Instant Digital Access)
4. **PDF Redact & Sensitive Data Masking Tool (`/tools/pdf-redactor` - ₹199)**:
   - DPDP Act 2023 compliant client-side privacy tool.
   - Automatically masks PAN, first 8 digits of Aadhaar, bank accounts, and signatures before sharing PDFs.
5. **Trial Balance to Balance Sheet & P&L Formatter (`/tools/tb-to-balancesheet` - ₹299)**:
   - Drag-and-drop raw Trial Balance Excel/CSV files.
   - Automatically groups ledgers into Schedule III Division I/II Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss statement.
6. **GSTR-2A vs Books Reconciliation Engine (`/tools/gstr2a-reconciliation` - ₹299)**:
   - 4-way matching of Books Purchase Register with Government GST 2A/2B records.
   - Detects ITC leakage, missing vendor invoices, and tax discrepancies.
7. **JSON to Tax Computation Sheet Formatter (`/tools/json-to-computation` - ₹199)**:
   - Converts raw government ITR JSON utility files into an executive, client-ready Tax Computation PDF.

   - Cleans messy multi-sheet GST files into an aggregated single-sheet vendor summary.
